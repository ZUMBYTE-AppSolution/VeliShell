New in VeliShell 0.13.8:

- The menu bar now meets the desktop seamlessly, removing the thin gap below it.
- The dock uses real blurred background glass instead of an artificial highlight line. Desktop colors show through while the corners remain neatly rounded.
- The menu bar, context menus, and floating panels share the more transparent glass style in light and dark modes. A readable fallback remains available where the effect is unsupported.
