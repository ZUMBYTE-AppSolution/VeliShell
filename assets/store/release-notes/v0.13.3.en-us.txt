New in VeliShell 0.13.3:

The menu bar can now show detected notification-area icons from running Windows apps. They appear in a monochrome style that adapts to the light or dark theme. Individual icons can be tucked behind an expandable chevron; the overflow closes after ten seconds without a selection or when it loses focus.

Shadows around the Dock, panels, previews, and VeliShell windows now look consistently soft. Rounded window outlines are no longer interrupted by rectangular shadow edges.

Note: When the Windows taskbar is hidden, third-party notification-area icons are mirrored on a best-effort basis. This does not replace their original context menus.
